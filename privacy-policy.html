Privacy Policy

Application: MultiChannel Inbox

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

MultiChannel Inbox is an application designed to help users manage communications through integrated services such as Gmail and WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the application may process:

  • Name and email address.
  • WhatsApp phone numbers and profile information.
  • Email messages and related information.
  • WhatsApp messages and delivery status information.
  • Authentication tokens required to connect supported services.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Display and manage messages in one interface.
  • Send and receive messages through connected services.
  • Show message delivery and read statuses.
  • Maintain and improve application functionality.
  • Resolve technical issues and protect the application.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may connect with third-party services such as Google Gmail API and Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Their respective privacy policies and terms also apply when you use those services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by integrated third-party services when necessary to provide the requested functionality or when required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Users may contact us to request information about their data or request its deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
